Frequently asked questions

Does AceBudget require an account or bank login?

No. AceBudget never asks for a bank password or any credential. There is no account, no server, and no sign-up. All data stays in your browser's local storage on your own device.

Does AceBudget work with cash and mobile money, not just bank cards?

Yes. Cash, mobile money, bank transfer, and card are all first-class payment methods on every transaction. AceBudget does not assume a card-rail economy.

What is Runway Days?

Runway Days is how many days a household could sustain its essential spending from liquid savings if income stopped today. It is AceBudget's north-star metric.

Does AceBudget work offline?

Yes. AceBudget is a zero-dependency, zero-build web app with an offline-capable service worker, so it keeps working without a connection once loaded.

Is AceBudget free?

Yes, AceBudget is free with no subscription tier and no paywalled features.

Is AceBudget a licensed financial advisor?

No. AceBudget is a planning and record-keeping tool, not a licensed financial adviser, and it does not provide personalised investment advice.
